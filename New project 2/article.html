How M-BMS supports NiMH hybrid battery health

Reliable hybrid battery behavior depends on good sensing, practical control logic, and clear service communication. VERA's M-BMS direction focuses on the embedded intelligence behind those outcomes.

Embedded intelligence for real operating conditions

M-BMS is positioned as an embedded system for Toyota and Lexus NiMH platforms. The system story is not only about hardware; it is about making battery behavior easier to understand, optimize, and support.

Why it matters

As hybrid platforms age, customers need technology that helps extend useful life and improve confidence. VERA combines automotive domain knowledge with energy-system engineering to support that need.
